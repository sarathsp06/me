# Accommodation Plan — Rotterdam to Switzerland Road Trip (May 2026)

3 adults + 2 children (5 and 1). One car. Prefer outside city centres, near motorways, good value.

---

## 1. Koblenz Area — 1 night (Sat 23 May → Sun 24 May)

**Type:** Hotel or guesthouse with family room

### Recommended areas (outside Koblenz city)

| Area | Drive to Koblenz | Why | Near motorway |
|------|-----------------|-----|---------------|
| **Lahnstein** | 5 min | Cheapest option near Koblenz, right at A61/A48 interchange. Still walkable to Rhine. | A61/A48 |
| **Vallendar** | 5 min north | Quiet Rhine-side town, close to Ehrenbreitstein cable car. | A48 |
| **Bendorf** | 10 min north | Very affordable guesthouses. Easy motorway access for Day 2 drive south. | A48 |

**Budget:** €70–120/night
**Search on Booking.com:** "Lahnstein" or "Vallendar", 23–24 May, 3 adults + 2 children (ages 5, 1)

## 2. Black Forest Area — 1 night (Sun 24 May → Mon 25 May)

**Type:** Gasthaus or pension with family room

### Recommended areas

| Area | Notes | Near motorway |
|------|-------|---------------|
| **Bühl or Achern** | On the A5, just south of Baden-Baden. Cheapest option. Easy motorway access for Day 3 drive to Switzerland. | A5 |
| **Freudenstadt** | Classic Black Forest town, on B28/A81 corridor. More character, still affordable. Family-friendly market square. | B28/A81 |
| **Baiersbronn** | 15 min from Freudenstadt. Traditional Black Forest village with Gasthaus half-board options. | B28 |

**Budget:** €60–100/night
**Search on Booking.com:** "Bühl" or "Freudenstadt", 24–25 May, 3 adults + 2 children (ages 5, 1)

## 3. Lake Lucerne — 5 nights (Mon 25 May → Sat 30 May)

**Type:** 2-bedroom apartment with kitchen (essential for 5 nights with kids — self-catering saves a lot in Switzerland)

### Recommended areas

| Area | Drive to Weggis boat | Why | Price level |
|------|---------------------|-----|-------------|
| **Weggis** | 0 min (walk) | Lakeside, walking distance to boats and Rigi cable car. More restaurants. First choice if budget allows. | €€€ |
| **Vitznau** | 5 min | Quieter, cogwheel railway to Rigi starts here. Slightly cheaper than Weggis. | €€€ |
| **Küssnacht am Rigi** | 10 min | Still on the lake, noticeably cheaper. Good car access. | €€ |
| **Brunnen** | 20 min | South side of lake. Often cheaper. Beautiful setting. | €€ |
| **Stans or Stansstad** | 15 min | Near Pilatus gondola side. Significantly cheaper. | € |
| **Horw or Kriens** | 15 min | Suburbs of Lucerne, near Pilatus gondola and A2 motorway. Cheapest option with car access to everything. | € |

**Budget:** CHF 150–220/night (€150–220)
**Search on Booking.com:** "Weggis" or "Küssnacht am Rigi" or "Horw", 25–30 May, 3 adults + 2 children (ages 5, 1), filter: apartments

### Notes

- Apartments with a kitchen are critical here — eating out in Switzerland is CHF 25–40/meal per adult.
- Staying in Horw/Kriens can save CHF 50–80/night vs Weggis, and you have a car.
- Wherever you stay, the lake boats connect everything — drive to the nearest boat pier and leave the car.

## Booking Tips

- **Book Lake Lucerne first** — the Lauerz apartment at €685 is the only sub-€200/night option and will go fast.
- **Filter by "free cancellation"** on Booking.com — plans can shift with kids.
- **Check for parking** — most rural places include free parking, but confirm for Swiss lakeside properties.
- **German stays**: Breakfast included is common at Gasthäuser — saves morning hassle.
- **Swiss stays**: Kitchen is the priority. Self-catering saves €30–50/day easily.
- **Prices are for 3 adults + 2 children (ages 5, 1)** — includes taxes and fees as shown on Booking.com.
